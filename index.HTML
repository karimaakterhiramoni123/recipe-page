Chocolate Cake Recipe

A simple recipe to bake the perfect chocolate cake at home.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour 3/4 cup
  • Cocoa powder 3/4 cup
  • Sugar 1.5 teaspoon
  • Baking powder 1.5 teaspoon
  • Baking soda 1.5 teaspoon
  • Salt 1 teaspoon
  • Eggs 2 pieces
  • Milk 1 cup
  • Oil or melted butte 1/2
  • Vanilla extract 2Vanilla extract
  • Hot water or hot coffee 1 cup

Instructions

  1. Mix all dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. Add eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla extract, then whisk well.
  3. Pour the smooth batter into a baking pan.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 30 to 35 minutes
